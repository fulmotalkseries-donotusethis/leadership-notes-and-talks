## 📌 Executive Overview & Mission

The **Fulmo Talk Series** is a non-profit, global platform designed to deliver short, science-backed, and inspiring talks created primarily for college and university students. Executed as a low-cost, open-access educational initiative, its primary mission is to democratize research in **mental wellness, subjective well-being, happiness, and productivity**.

### 🎯 Core Mission & Social Impact
* **Addressing Student Mental Health:** The platform was established with a dedicated focus on addressing the student mental health crisis and supporting suicide prevention across university campuses in India, Australia, and the United States.
* **Open & Accessible Learning:** All recorded sessions, accompanying reading lists, and curated positive psychology materials are made freely available to students globally—removing financial barriers to mental health resources.
* **Frugal & Autonomy-Driven Technology:** Backed by **Fulmo Frugal Tech**, the platform promotes transparent, open-access mental wellness tools and open resources, rejecting proprietary subscriptions or closed-ecosystem hype in favor of critical thinking and user autonomy.

## 🧠 Philosophy: The "Deep Generalist" vs. "Jack of All Trades"

In modern academic and corporate discourse, people frequently speak of a *"jack of all trades, master of none."* However, there is a massive structural gap between a surface-level generalist and a **competent Deep Generalist**—someone who achieves genuine domain expertise across multiple distinct disciplines.

Building and hosting the Fulmo Talk Series required drawing upon cross-disciplinary mastery built over 25+ years across systems architecture, computer science, financial strategy, cognitive research, and history:

* **High-Level Intellectual Engagement:** Hosting, presenting, and engaging with top-tier global experts—ranging from world-renowned psychologists and Harvard Medical School faculty to visionary rail engineers and eco-artists—requires deeply understanding and critiquing peak-level contributions across varied domains.
* **Cross-Pollination of Ideas:** Engaging directly with the peak achievements of leaders in diverse fields led to the synthesis of original, highly interdisciplinary frameworks—bridging ancient philosophy, computational thinking, and positive psychology.
* **Artisanal Problem Solving:** Approaching cross-disciplinary challenges like an artisan, with care, originality, and a core belief that high-impact frameworks must be slow-brewed, rigorous, and meaningful.

## 🌟 Featured Speakers & Keynote Talks (2025 & 2026 Editions)

The 2025 and 2026 series brought together a distinguished roster of global thought leaders, academics, and industry pioneers:

### 1. 🧠 Prof. Sonja Lyubomirsky
* **Background:** Distinguished Professor of Psychology at UC Riverside; Templeton Positive Psychology Prize winner; Harvard & Stanford alumna; best-selling author of *The How of Happiness*.
* **Talk Title:** *How to Be Happier, Backed by Science*
* **Core Themes:** Science-backed strategies to cultivate well-being through intentional daily actions—focusing on social connection, gratitude, kindness, and radical curiosity through meaningful 15-minute conversations.

### 2. 🏛️ Prof. Anand Manikutty
* **Background:** CXO, Software Architect, Educator, Researcher, and Former Editor of *Termite* (high-IQ society journal); Stanford & Harvard Business School alumnus.
* **Talk Title:** *Designing Joy: Ancient Tamil Wisdom, Gxoja Filozofio & Computational Thinking*
* **Core Themes:** Integrates ancient Tamil literature, happiness research, and computational thinking. Introduces **La Joya Filozofio** (The Happy Philosophy)—a novel philosophy designed as a rival to ancient Stoicism/Epicureanism and modern Existentialism—while challenging colonial readings of South Asian history. *(Sponsored in association with IEEE and ACM Societies)*.

### 3. 🔬 Prof. Sanjiv Chopra
* **Background:** Professor of Medicine and former Faculty Dean for Continuing Medical Education at Harvard Medical School; best-selling author.
* **Talk Title:** *The Science of Longevity and Purpose*
* **Core Themes:** Evidence-based medical insights on living a healthier, purpose-driven, and meaningful life.

### 4. 🚆 Dr. Sudhanshu Mani
* **Background:** Visionary engineer and architect of India's iconic Vande Bharat Express; leader in rail technology, heritage, and visual arts.
* **Talk Title:** *Innovation, Heritage, and Engineering Leadership*
* **Core Themes:** Technical innovation, environmental stewardship, and public space transformation through creative design.

### 5. 💻 Prof. Murali Mani
* **Background:** Professor of Computer Science at the University of Michigan-Flint; expert in big data management and analytics.
* **Talk Title:** *Information Consumption & Social Media Architectures*
* **Core Themes:** Examining how modern social media architectures reshape news consumption and computer science education.

### 6. 🌿 Mr. Vishwanath Mallabadi
* **Background:** Bangalore-based contemporary eco-artist and sustainability advocate with 30+ years in technology and upcycling.
* **Talk Title:** *Obsolete But Not Forgotten: Tech in Agripreneurship & Eco-Art*
* **Core Themes:** Transforming electronic waste (e-waste) into public art; environmental responsibility and sustainable upcycling.

### 7. 🏋️ Mr. Dave Sheahan
* **Background:** High-performance coach and wellness educator.
* **Talk Title:** *Mind, Body, and Sustainable High Performance*
* **Core Themes:** Actionable strategies for physical/mental control as the foundational catalyst for career, health, and relationship success.

### 8. ⚡ Dr. Jacob Verghese
* **Background:** Energy expert, Indian Railways veteran, and Founder Director of Steag Energy Services India.
* **Talk Title:** *Energy Systems & Strategic Infrastructure*
* **Core Themes:** Sustainable energy infrastructure, technical leadership, and global project execution.
