# README

### Talk on Designing Joy -- Fulmo Talk Series

#### Summary
In this important talk, Anand Manikutty — a Top 100 Ranker in India’s Joint Entrance Examination, a World Record holder in Software performance and a former Editor of Termite magazine — brings together ancient Tamil literature, happiness research, and computational thinking. Drawing from a new developed Philosophy La Joya Filozofio, he challenges colonial readings of Indian philosophy as well as history, and offers a bold, modern reinterpretation.

The “Designing Joy” Prelego (trans. : “Talk”) is a talk for anyone curious about the science of well-being, and the wisdom of the past. This Videa prelego (trans. : “Video Talk”) was prepared as an adjunct to a Talk given by Prof. Anand Manikutty sponsored by the IEEE Society and the ACM Society. This Prelego is subtitled Gxoja Filozofio, Ancient Tamil Wisdom, and Computational Thinking. In addition to introducing a new way of looking at South Asian History, he has also developed a new Philosophy, called La Joya Filozofio, which is meant to be a rival to ancient philosophies, such as Stoicism and Epicureanism, as well as modern philosophies, such as Existentialism.
